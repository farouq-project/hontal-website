## Photography guidelines

- **Resolution:** 1920 × 1080px minimum (retina: 2560 × 1440px ideal)
- **Format:** PNG or WebP. WebP preferred for smaller file size.
- **Blur:** Blur or mask any real customer names, phone numbers, or addresses before uploading.
- **UI authenticity:** Keep the Hontal interface authentic — do not edit or fake data beyond blurring.
- **Compression:** Run through TinyPNG or Squoosh before uploading. Target under 500KB per file.

## What each screenshot should show

### hero-dashboard.png
The full Hontal dashboard. This is the most important image on the page.
Show the Dispatch Board or main overview screen with enough data to look "live" —
20–30 orders in various states (pending, assigned, delivered), multiple drivers visible.

### dispatch-board.png
The Dispatch Board in full operation.
Show 20–40 orders across statuses: Pending, Assigned, In Transit, Completed.
Multiple drivers listed. Order cards with details visible (but blur customer names/addresses).

### driver-live-tracking.png
The live map or driver tracking view.
Show driver pins on a map, route lines, delivery stops.
Real Indonesian city map preferred (Jakarta, Surabaya, or Jawa geography).

### customer-intelligence.png
The Customer Intelligence or Segmentation dashboard.
Show the 5-tier breakdown: New, Active, At-Risk, Dormant, Lost.
Customer list visible but names blurred.
Charts or segment counts visible.

### business-health-score.png
The Business Health Score screen.
Show the composite score (any number 65–95/100 looks credible).
The 5 dimension breakdown visible.
Goal progress indicators if available.
